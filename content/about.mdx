# Intelligent systems meet human design.
 
I'm a senior AI product designer who makes complex systems legible to the people who depend on them. My focus is physical and agentic AI: the drones, robots, and machine-state interfaces where a person has to read what a system is doing, trust it, and act, often under pressure. I design the layer where intelligence becomes something an operator can actually understand. Machine state made readable. Ambiguity resolved before it turns into a mistake.
 
<AboutPhoto />
Most systems get designed around how engineers assume operators think, not how people actually reason when the stakes are high and emotions are loud. This is where my second craft comes in. Two decades of design, plus years of coaching, taught me to suspend my assumptions and listen for the real problem beneath the stated one. I design from how people make decisions under load, not from an idealized user who never exists. Curiosity over certainty, every time.
 
The result is systems people can trust: clear when it counts, honest about their own state, and built for the human who has to rely on them.

Physical AI. Systems that move through the world and report back on what they found, where a person has to act on that report with real consequences attached. Setting design direction for that work, building the team that delivers it, and making the case for it where it gets funded.

If you're building autonomy that has to work in someone's hands, not just perform on a benchmark, that's the problem I'm built for.
